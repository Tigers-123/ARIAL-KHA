ARIALKHA AGRO — Design System

Concept name: "Paddy Row Editorial". Version 0.1 — visual direction for the storefront.

1 · The Concept

  • Editorial, not e-commerce template. The site reads like a beautifully printed harvest journal — generous whitespace, serif display type, asymmetric grids, generous line-height. Rice is a heritage product; the UI borrows dignity from print, not from app UI kits.
  • The paddy-row motif. Fine horizontal line stacks (repeating-linear-gradient) appear between major sections — a fingerprint of terraced paddy fields. This replaces generic divider lines and section backgrounds. It is cheap to render, scales to any width, and is unmistakable once you see it.
  • Hand-made illustration instead of stock photography. All visuals on the homepage prototype are inline SVG (paddy terraces, a farmer silhouette at sunset, rice-sack product marks). Flat, warm, slightly imperfect — like block-printed packaging. Swap for real R2-hosted photography in production, keeping the same color treatment.

2 · The Aesthetic Risk (and its justification)

Risk: the primary CTA is mustard yellow, not green — and body ink is warm husk-brown, not black.

  • Every natural-brand site on earth makes green the action color. Green here instead does the brand work (trust, foliage, header, footer), while --sun #E2A52B does the conversion work. Mustard is the color of harvested paddy itself — it is literally the client's product — and it carries ≥4.5:1 contrast against both cream and deep green when paired with dark text (#3A2A05 on #E2A52B ≈ 8.5:1).
  • Pure black (#000) is banned. Text uses #2B2618 — rice-husk brown — which keeps long-form Bangla reading soft and warm, and reinforces "paper & grain" rather than "screen & SaaS".
  • A supporting risk: oversized Bengali glyphs ("ধান", "মাটি, পানি, রোদ") used as decorative background art inside an English-first layout. Justified because the brand is bilingual by mandate — Bangla is treated as a first-class visual citizen, not a translated afterthought.

5 · Signature Motif — The Paddy Row

Pure CSS: repeating-linear-gradient, 1px lines at 6.5px rhythm, edge-masked. Zero images, zero JS, instant paint. Used between sections instead of whitespace or stock texture.

9 · Rules That Keep It Distinctive

  • Ban pure black, pure white backgrounds beyond cards, neon anything, glassmorphism.
  • Max one accent color per screen region.
  • Every crossed-out price must map to a real, dated campaign (enforced server-side later).
  • Bangla text never uses a Latin font fallback mid-sentence — split strings at the CMS layer.
  • Motion is limited to ≤300ms, translate/scale only, and fully disabled under prefers-reduced-motion.
  • Images carry the warm duotone treatment (paddy greens + mustard) — no raw stock-photo color.
